### Research Focus

I develop automated nonlinear finite-element workflows, custom numerical extensions, and interpretable surrogate models to quantify structural performance, hysteretic energy dissipation, residual deformation, and uncertainty in complex civil systems.

* **Computational Mechanics & FEM:** 3D nonlinear dynamic modeling in OpenSees/OpenSeesPy and ABAQUS, soil–structure interaction (SSI), and FRP composite retrofit modeling.
* **In-House Tools & Scientific Computing:** Custom C++ OpenSees recorders and automated Python, MATLAB, and Tcl parametric simulation pipelines.
* **Sensitivity & Surrogate Modeling:** Latin hypercube sampling (LHS), Lasso regularization, cross-validation, uncertainty quantification, and multivariable demand/fragility modeling.
